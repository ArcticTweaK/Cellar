Cellar 1.1 is a big update: a cleaner window, a much better Launch tab, and a lot of tools for running many
accounts. Your accounts, settings and presets carry over by themselves.

## Easier to use

- **One screen for everything** - your accounts on the left, every tool one tab away (Launch, Presets, Macros,
  Anti-AFK & rejoin, Windows, My Sober, Performance, Settings, Help) and the log at the bottom.
- **Themes** - Blossom, Midnight, Mint, Ember, Graphite, Daylight, or your desktop's own look, plus your own
  accent colour. **Size** (Settings > Appearance) makes everything bigger or smaller.
- **Ctrl+K** opens a command palette: type a few letters of an account, a tool, a preset or a game.
- **First-run setup** checks your system and shows the exact install command for anything missing.
- Cellar tells you when a new version is out (once a day, can be turned off).

## Launching

- **The Launch tab** - pick where accounts go: a **Game** (your recent games and favourites as big tiles, or
  search / paste a link), a **Server** (Quick join picks the emptiest one, or a busy one with room for
  everyone), a saved **Private server**, or a **Friend** (shows who is online and what they play). The launch
  button says exactly what will happen.
- **A launch list** shows each account starting, with the reason if one fails, and **Retry failed**.
- **Play on roblox.com opens Cellar** (turn it on in Settings > Launching): pick which accounts join.
- Right-click an account > **Bring accounts to its server** sends your alts into the exact server it is in.
- **Schedules** - start a preset at a time of day and stop it later (overnight works).
- Command line: `cellar launch`, `cellar stop`, `cellar list`, `cellar preset`.

## Running many accounts

- **Kicks noticed in seconds** from each account's Roblox log, with a notification and an automatic rejoin.
- **Freeze** an alt (0% CPU) without closing it - it wakes now and then so it stays in the server.
- **Mute alts**, or give each account its own volume.
- **Screen wall** - live pictures of every background account in one window.
- **Macros** - an editor that underlines mistakes, `repeat` / `random` / `stop after` blocks, import / export,
  and **Step by step** to run a macro one line at a time and see where it goes wrong.
- **History** - which account played what, for how long, and the time played in the last 7 days.
- **Window layouts** on KDE Plasma, Hyprland and Sway; saved layouts, and spreading windows over all monitors.
- CPU and memory per account, graphs of the last hour, and RAM-aware launching.
- **Discord alerts** now include a picture of a background account's screen when it gets disconnected.

## Accounts

- **Account switcher** - right-click > *Use in my normal Sober*.
- **Colour tags**, pin to top, drag and drop to reorder, bulk edit, cards or table view.
- Export your accounts to a password-protected file, import them on another PC, or restore a backup.
- Drop cookie files or an accounts file onto the window to add them.
- **Auto-lock** (with a password on your accounts file): Cellar hides your accounts after a while in the
  background; your accounts keep running.

## Your normal Sober

- **My Sober** - Sober's settings in plain words, FPS cap, a Fast Flags editor that shows which flags Roblox
  really uses, and cleanup of caches and crash dumps.
- **Roblox logs** - what happened in a session (joined, left, disconnected and why, real errors) with a
  *Copy for a bug report* that removes your cookies.

## Fixes

- Right-click menus and windows no longer pile up in memory during long sessions.
- A macro's `type` step keeps text after a `#`; repeated "window closed" Discord alerts while offline are gone.
- Many smaller fixes.

If something goes wrong: Settings > **System check** > *Copy report* gives you a bug report with your cookies
removed - paste it into an issue on GitHub.
